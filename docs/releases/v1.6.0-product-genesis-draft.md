## What changes

Product Genesis gives a user who starts with only a rough idea a durable,
professional path through discovery, experiments, business viability, privacy
and security assurance, approved business requirements, business rules,
specification, design, capacity-bounded iterations, production readiness,
operation, outcome review, and retirement.

The local Product Workspace preserves immutable artifact versions and current
heads. Exact hashes bind named-human approvals, requirement convergence,
evidence receipts, environment attestations, and limited or production release
candidates. The agent can recommend `continue`, `pivot`, `pause`, or `stop`, but
cannot approve a baseline or claim provider and production evidence by itself.
Repository-bound evidence preserves the forge hostname as well as repository
path. GitHub issue apply additionally requires a repository-trusted, short-lived
Ed25519 `MEMBER` action with `product.github.write`; a durable one-use nonce is
consumed before any remote call.

## Honest boundaries

- Local and synthetic evidence does not prove customer demand, legal approval,
  provider behavior, deployed availability, production capacity, or business
  outcomes.
- Product Genesis supplies the process, durable artifacts, validation, and
  gates. Real customer research, accountable business/legal/security owners,
  provider evidence, deployment, operations, and live telemetry remain required.
- The bundled Product Workspace is local repository state, not a hosted
  multi-user product-management service.
