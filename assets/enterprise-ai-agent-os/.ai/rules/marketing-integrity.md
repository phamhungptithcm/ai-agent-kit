# Marketing Integrity Rules

These rules apply to positioning, public copy, landing pages, funnels, calls to action, conversion work, analytics, experiments, localization, campaigns, outbound content, paid media, and public proof.

## Truth And Proof

- Classify material inputs as verified, assumption, or unknown and keep a reviewable source for verified public claims.
- Never fabricate or imply customers, testimonials, logos, ratings, reviews, awards, press, partnerships, certifications, credentials, case studies, usage, performance, scarcity, demand, prices, savings, or outcomes.
- Do not use a third-party benchmark, correlation, heuristic, or percentage as a universal rule or guaranteed result.
- Do not promise traffic, ranking, leads, conversion, revenue, attribution, or campaign performance.
- Use `NOT_MEASURED` when baseline or outcome data is unavailable. Missing data is not zero and an unobserved outcome is not success.
- Keep projections and recommendations distinct from observed results.

## User Choice And Experience

- Do not use fake urgency, fake scarcity, deceptive countdowns, hidden costs, confirmshaming, forced continuity, misdirection, obstruction, or other dark patterns.
- Keep the offer, terms, price, renewal, cancellation, data use, and CTA consequence understandable before commitment.
- Do not optimize a local metric by harming accessibility, privacy, security, performance, content meaning, user control, or downstream outcome quality.
- Preserve truthful message match from source or campaign through landing page, CTA, and next state.
- Treat localization as market, meaning, legal, cultural, layout, and search adaptation rather than word substitution alone.
- Treat competitor and campaign references as research, not permission to copy distinctive copy, creative, identity, data, or protected assets.
- Identify required legal, policy, platform, and subject-matter review without claiming compliance from a generic checklist.

## Measurement And Privacy

- Define the decision, metric semantics, source of truth, population, period, consent, retention, and limitations before adding tracking.
- Collect the minimum data needed for the stated decision. Do not send secrets, sensitive free text, authentication data, or unnecessary identifiers to analytics or advertising systems.
- Distinguish correlation, attribution, modeled estimates, and causal evidence.
- Do not claim an experiment won without valid instrumentation, eligibility, baseline, decision rules, and adequate evidence.
- Preserve consent choices and equivalent core access where tracking or personalization is declined unless a lawful requirement says otherwise.

## External Actions

- A plan, brief, draft, or implementation approval does not authorize publishing, sending, enabling tracking, mutating accounts, launching experiments, or spending budget.
- Route paid media, outbound messages, external publishing, account mutations, production tracking changes, and budget actions through the governed action gateway with current explicit authorization.
- Keep copy-ready output separate from executed external actions and record execution evidence only when verified.
- Treat audience-list upload, enrichment, matching, scraping, and retargeting as protected data actions requiring purpose, authority, minimization, and applicable consent or legal review.
