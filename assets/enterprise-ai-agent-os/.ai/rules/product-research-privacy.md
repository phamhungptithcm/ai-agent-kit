# Product Research Privacy

- Classify idea, customer, interview, competitive, personal, credential, and regulated data before research.
- Minimize data and redact identifiers where they are not needed.
- Do not upload confidential content, recordings, customer lists, or unpublished strategy to external tools without explicit authority.
- Record consent, permitted use, retention, and deletion expectations for interviews and recordings.
- Treat public availability as a sourcing fact, not permission to republish sensitive personal data.
- Record which external services received which data.
