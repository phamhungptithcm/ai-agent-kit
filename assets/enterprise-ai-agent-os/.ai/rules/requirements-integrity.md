# Requirements Integrity

- Give every requirement a stable identifier, owner, rationale, priority, status, source, acceptance method, and trace links.
- Use testable language with actors, conditions, behavior, thresholds, units, and scope.
- Separate business need from candidate implementation.
- Keep goals, requirements, assumptions, constraints, risks, decisions, and non-goals distinct.
- Reject vague terms such as fast, intuitive, scalable, secure, and user-friendly unless measurable criteria define them.
- Detect duplicates, conflicts, missing failure paths, orphan requirements, and requirements without verification.
- A `TBD` must name an owner and resolution gate; it is not silent approval.
