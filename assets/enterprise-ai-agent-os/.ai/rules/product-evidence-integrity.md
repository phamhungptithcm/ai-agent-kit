# Product Evidence Integrity

- Label each material statement as observed fact, sourced fact, user statement, assumption, inference, or unknown.
- Attach source, retrieval date, method, scope, and confidence to research claims.
- Direct user evidence, desk research, prototype feedback, synthetic tests, staging evidence, and production evidence are not interchangeable.
- Preserve contradictory and negative evidence.
- Missing evidence is unavailable, never positive, zero-cost, validated, or approved.
- Record successor versions instead of rewriting historical artifacts.
