# Human Writing Integrity Rules

These rules apply when prose is rewritten to sound more natural, authentic,
personal, or less model-generated.

## Preserve Substance

- Preserve material meaning, facts, uncertainty, chronology, attribution,
  citations, quotations, asks, promises, disclaimers, and emotional intent.
- Never invent personal experience, anecdotes, numbers, names, outcomes,
  credentials, relationships, quotes, sources, or opinions to add texture.
- Do not strengthen certainty, urgency, blame, praise, authority, or commitment
  beyond the source.
- Keep normative terms, defined terms, code, commands, and contractual or legal
  meaning unchanged unless the user explicitly approves a substantive edit.
- Flag ambiguity when a stylistic change could alter interpretation.

## Respect Voice And Context

- Follow the requested audience, channel, locale, length, formality, and voice.
- Use supplied writing samples only to infer observable style for the current
  task. Do not infer identity, demographics, beliefs, or private facts.
- Do not make every writer sound casual, punchy, vulnerable, or opinionated.
- Treat pattern lists and numeric thresholds as heuristics, not universal tests.
- Avoid secondary convergence after removing repetitive language or structure.

## Protect Authorship And Trust

- Do not remove attribution, obscure copied work, or disguise plagiarism.
- Do not impersonate an identifiable person deceptively or add experiences the
  represented author did not have.
- Do not claim that rewritten content was created without AI assistance.
- Do not promise to evade AI detectors or use a detector score as proof of human
  authorship or writing quality.
- Preserve required disclosures, citations, and provenance.

## Protect Sensitive Writing

- Treat personal samples, unpublished drafts, internal communications, and
  voice profiles as task-scoped data.
- Do not place them in logs, examples, fixtures, telemetry, or durable memory.
- Redact or generalize sensitive details only when requested or required by the
  applicable privacy policy, and disclose any material meaning loss.

## Output Discipline

- Return the rewrite directly unless the user asks for rationale, alternatives,
  tracked changes, or a review.
- Do not add emojis, hashtags, engagement bait, headings, or calls to action
  unless requested or already supported by the source.
- Keep technical, academic, legal, regulated, and structured content outside the
  default scope; when explicitly requested, prioritize precision over style.
- Report unresolved factual or voice questions instead of guessing.
