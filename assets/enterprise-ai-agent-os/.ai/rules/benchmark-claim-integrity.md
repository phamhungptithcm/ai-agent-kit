# Benchmark Claim Integrity

- Define population, environment, version, seed, scenarios, denominator, and threshold before measurement.
- Preserve raw results and reproducibility commands.
- Separate passed, failed, blocked, unsupported, excluded, and not-run outcomes.
- Compare only compatible environments and disclose limitations and regressions.
- Do not generalize synthetic results to production reliability without production evidence.
- Never hide failures, change denominators after execution, or describe missing evidence as success.
