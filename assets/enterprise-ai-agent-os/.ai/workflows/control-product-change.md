# Control Product Change Workflow

1. Capture the requested change, trigger, urgency, and affected baseline.
2. Trace impact across business outcomes, requirements, journeys, contracts, architecture, data, security, backlog, tests, operations, cost, and schedule.
3. Classify the change as clarification, non-material correction, or material change.
4. Present options, recommendation, migration, compatibility, rollback, and work-pause boundary.
5. Create successor documents and an old-to-new trace map.
6. Obtain reapproval for every materially affected baseline.
7. Resume only the scope that remains authorized.
