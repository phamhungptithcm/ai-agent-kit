# Review Product Outcome Workflow

1. Resolve the approved baseline and delivered scope.
2. Verify acceptance, security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, performance, data, operational, support, cost, rollout, and rollback evidence.
3. Distinguish design, local, synthetic, staging, pilot, and production proof.
4. Compare actual user and business outcomes against baselines and targets.
5. Record gaps, incidents, feedback, unintended effects, and invalidated assumptions.
6. Make a bounded `RELEASE`, `LIMITED_RELEASE`, `HOLD`, `ROLLBACK`, `ITERATE`, or `RETIRE` recommendation.
7. Create the next evidence, change, or discovery version without rewriting history.
