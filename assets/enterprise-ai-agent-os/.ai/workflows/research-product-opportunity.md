# Research Product Opportunity Workflow

1. Classify sensitive information and establish what may leave the repository.
2. Convert unknowns into research questions, decision thresholds, methods, owners, and stopping conditions.
3. Collect the smallest useful mix of user, workflow, market, alternative, official, technical, cost, privacy, legal, and operational evidence.
4. Record every source in the evidence ledger; preserve raw notes separately from synthesis.
5. Triangulate claims and explicitly record contradictions, sampling limits, freshness, and confidence.
6. Evaluate desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, safety, and operability.
7. Recommend `PROCEED`, `PIVOT`, `PAUSE`, or `STOP`, with falsifying evidence and next decision.

No external publication, paid research, outreach, recording, or confidential upload is implied by this workflow.
